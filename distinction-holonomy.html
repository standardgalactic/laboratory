Model correspondence
This implements the repository's explicit ℝ² toy model: A = X dx + Y dy, with X = [[0,1],[0,0]] and Y = [[0,0],[1,0]]. The former proportional-correction control has been removed because it was an illustrative damping rule, not the model's gauge-invariant repair functional.
