\chapter{Every Register}
\label{app:registers}

Every register covered in Chapter~\ref{ch:registers}, addressed with a leading \texttt{"} in Normal mode or \texttt{@} in Vimscript.

\begin{longtable}{@{}l p{9.5cm}@{}}
\textbf{Register} & \textbf{Holds} \\
\hline
\endhead
\texttt{"} (unnamed) & Most recent deletion, change, or yank \\
\texttt{a}--\texttt{z} & Named registers; lowercase overwrites, uppercase (\texttt{A}--\texttt{Z}) appends \\
\texttt{0} & Most recent yank specifically (unaffected by subsequent deletions) \\
\texttt{1}--\texttt{9} & Automatically shifted history of line-or-larger deletions \\
\texttt{-} (small delete) & Most recent deletion smaller than one line \\
\texttt{:} & Most recently executed Ex command (read-only) \\
\texttt{/} & Most recent search pattern (read-only) \\
\texttt{.} & Most recently inserted text (read-only) \\
\texttt{\%} & Current file name (read-only) \\
\texttt{\#} & Alternate file name (read-only) \\
\texttt{=} & Expression register; evaluates a typed Vimscript expression \\
\texttt{*} & System selection/clipboard (platform-dependent) \\
\texttt{+} & System clipboard (X11 systems specifically) \\
\texttt{\_} & Black hole register; discards on write, empty on read \\
\end{longtable}
