# Prose Style

Use long, coherent paragraphs when the argument benefits from continuity. Avoid excessive headings, slogans, bullet lists, and rhetorical questions.

Prefer:

- explicit transitions;
- operational definitions;
- careful distinctions;
- qualified claims;
- examples that test rather than merely decorate;
- clear statements of assumptions;
- revision over repetition.

Avoid:

- generic introductions;
- inflated claims of novelty;
- phrases such as “in today’s rapidly changing world”;
- empty summaries;
- invented citations;
- pretending uncertain claims are established;
- renaming canonical concepts for stylistic variety.
